ROADSTER: THRUST LAB

An unofficial cold-gas thruster test facility at night. One rocket-assisted Roadster, a tall curved rig, a clearance range, gravity well and collision lab. Run the experiments, thread the test gates, recover the data cores and break the crash blocks. Chain flips, spins and adhesion runs for combo multipliers. Pass the light gates to set checkpoints.

Use the left joystick to drive and steer. Pull back while turning to brake and drift; hold ROCKET or LIFT to thrust. Open Controls for the full guide.

W / S Accelerate, brake, reverse (pitch in air)
A / D Steer (roll in air)
S + A / D Brake while steering to drift
Space Rear thruster, uses fuel reserve
F · Z / C Bottom thrusters · left / right side thrusters
Shift Hold to engage wall grip
G Toggle grip system on/off, including automatic grip
M AUTO landing assist / MANUAL direct thruster control
W S · A D · Q E Pitch, roll, yaw in the air. Works in AUTO too: the autopilot levels the car before landing
R · T Respawn at your last checkpoint · back to the start
